# Short-Form Video Library — Photography Business

Vertical video specs (Reels / Shorts / TikTok). 15–40s each, strong hook, real value, simple visuals.

## VIDEO 1 — The Client Questionnaire Hook
- Hook (0-3s): "Send this one form to every client before you quote them — it eliminates 80% of problems."
- Content: 3-question teaser from the questionnaire (usage, budget, deadline).
- CTA: "Link in bio for the full 19-question questionnaire."
- Visual: Screen recording of the questionnaire form, voiceover.

## VIDEO 2 — Shoot-Day Checklist Hook
- Hook (0-3s): "I show up to every shoot with a 40-item checklist taped to my bag. Here's the top 5."
- Content: 5 must-do items from the checklist (deposit, gear check, test-fire, backups, client confirm).
- CTA: "Full checklist free on the site — link in bio."
- Visual: Checklist scrolling on phone, gear bag B-roll.

## VIDEO 3 — Pricing Anchor
- Hook (0-3s): "Most new photographers underprice by 40%. Here's the one calculation that fixes it."
- Content: Break-even rate walkthrough (time + gear + software ÷ sessions).
- CTA: "Free pricing calculator on the site."
- Visual: Spreadsheet screen recording, pen-and-paper math.

## VIDEO 4 — First Client No-Following
- Hook (0-3s): "You don't need Instagram followers to get paid photography work. Here's what actually works."
- Content: 3-step playbook (pick niche, 5-image portfolio, targeted outreach).
- CTA: "Full first-client guide linked."
- Visual: Phone mockup of outreach email, before/after portfolio grid.

## VIDEO 5 — Difficult Client Script
- Hook (0-3s): "The exact script I use when a client tries to add 'just one more shot' for free."
- Content: Scope creeper response, read from the contract language.
- CTA: "Full difficult-client guide + templates on the site."
- Visual: Phone screen showing the script, you reading it.

## VIDEO 6 — Gallery Delivery
- Hook (0-3s): "The one email I send after every gallery delivery that gets me rebooked."
- Content: 3-sentence delivery email template.
- CTA: "Full gallery delivery guide + email templates linked."
- Visual: Email on screen, gallery preview in background.
