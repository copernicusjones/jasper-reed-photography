Shoot-Day Checklist

Photography Business Resources · Free Download

3 Days Before

  • Confirm shoot date, time, and location with client (in writing)
  • Confirm exact deliverables and usage license with client
  • Deposit received (if not yet received, collect before shoot day)
  • Scout location or confirm venue access + parking
  • Check weather forecast (outdoor shoots) and agree on rain plan
  • Confirm models/third parties and their call times
  • Gear check: camera bodies, lenses, batteries, memory cards, lights, stands, modifiers, reflectors
  • Clean memory cards and format them
  • Charge ALL batteries (spares included)
  • Pack cables, card reader, spare batteries for every device
  • Confirm transportation and travel time (add 30 min buffer)

Night Before

  • Repack bag from the gear checklist (never assume)
  • Backup camera body + backup lens packed (if owned)
  • Memory cards: minimum 2× estimated need
  • Set two alarms
  • Map directions saved offline
  • Client contact number saved in phone
  • Shot list printed or saved to phone
  • Contract signed + deposit confirmed in folder/email (bring copies)

Shoot Day — Before

  • Arrive 15 minutes early (30 for new locations)
  • Walk the space: light, power outlets, backgrounds, traffic noise
  • Test-fire one frame on each camera body
  • Set white balance / custom WB for the space
  • Confirm with client: final shot list, must-have shots, people present
  • Check model releases are signed for anyone recognizable

During

  • Shoot the MUST-HAVE shots first (from the shot list)
  • Backup to second card (dual-slot cameras) or change cards at the halfway point
  • Review key frames on the back screen every 15–20 shots (focus, exposure, expression)
  • Ask client to review a few frames on camera — catches problems while you can still fix them
  • Log any special notes per shot (client preferences, retouch needs)

After — On Location

  • Do NOT delete anything from the cards
  • Copy cards to a laptop/portable drive if possible before leaving
  • Count cards, lenses, bodies against the packing list before you leave (nothing left behind)
  • Get any final client signatures (release forms, additional usage)

After — At Home / Studio

  • Copy all files to your main drive (two locations: internal + external/cloud)
  • Verify file counts: card count = folder count, nothing missed
  • Format cards ONLY after verified backup (never format first)
  • Log the shoot in your tracking sheet (client, date, hours, expenses, price)
  • Record actual expenses (travel, parking, meals) for your records
  • Send client a quick "got home safe, images are backed up" message — professional touch
  • Schedule culling/editing time in your calendar

Delivery

  • Cull to the agreed number of selects
  • Edit + export at agreed resolution/format
  • Upload to gallery/delivery link
  • Send invoice if balance is outstanding (balance due before delivery of finals)
  • Follow up if no download within 7 days
  • Request a testimonial (only after successful delivery and full payment)
