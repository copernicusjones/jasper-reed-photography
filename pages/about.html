About Jasper Reed Photography & Automation

I run a photography business and I build the systems that keep it running. This site is the public half of that work — the free resources I wish I'd had starting out, and the fixed-price services I take on for other photographers and small businesses.

What this business actually does: two things, plainly. Photography business operations, and paid data/automation work delivered on a fixed price. Everything is handled in writing and delivered asynchronously. There are no consultation calls.

Background

My work sits at the crossing point of photography and operations. On the photography side that means client intake, shoot-day process, pricing, gallery delivery, and the client-management problems nobody warns you about. On the operations side it means the unglamorous work that actually costs businesses money when it's wrong: messy spreadsheet exports, product data that won't import, metadata that doesn't meet an agency standard, and websites that break in the one flow a customer was trying to complete.

That's why the first services I offer are the ones I've had to solve for myself. I'm not reselling someone else's process — these are workflows I built because I needed them.
