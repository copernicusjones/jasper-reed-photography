Client Questionnaire

Photography Business Resources · Free Download

About the Client

  1. What is your name, business name (if any), and website/Instagram?
  2. Who is this shoot for — you personally, your business, or a client of yours?
  3. What are you using the photos for? (social media, website, ads, print, portfolio, personal)
  4. Where will the photos be seen? (Instagram, website, billboard, magazine, product packaging, etc.)
  5. How soon do you need the final images?

About the Shoot

  1. What exactly would you like photographed? Describe the products, people, space, or event.
  2. How many items / shots do you estimate? (If products: how many individual products?)
  3. Where will the shoot happen — your location or mine? Address?
  4. Do you have a preferred date and time? Any flexibility?
  5. Do you have examples of photos you love? (Links or screenshots — this is the single most useful answer.)
  6. Is there a specific style you DON'T want?
  7. Are there any must-have shots? List them.
  8. Will anyone else be involved? (models, staff, family, pets — list who)

Usage & Licensing

  1. How long do you need to use these photos? (a few months, a year, forever)
  2. Do you need exclusive rights, or is it OK if I use the photos in my own portfolio?
  3. Will the images be used to sell products or services (commercial use), or just to share/show them (personal use)?

Logistics

  1. What's your budget range? (Be honest — this saves everyone time. Ranges are fine.)
  2. How did you hear about me?
  3. Anything else I should know? (timing constraints, access issues, special requests)

After they answer, you should be able to produce: a concrete quote, the right license, the right shot list, and zero surprises on shoot day. If an answer is missing, ask for it — don't guess.
